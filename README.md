# Six Words, One Story

Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Six Words, One Story"**
(`u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410`).

## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes one complete story
told in exactly six words to `/output/entry.txt`. The program is fully
deterministic: it always writes the same six-word story.

## The story

> She waited fifty years. He knocked.

Six words, 33 characters. A lifetime of waiting, and the turn: after half
a century, he is at the door. Why he left, why now, and whether she opens
the door are left for the reader to fill in.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
